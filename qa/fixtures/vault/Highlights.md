Obsidian 1.14 colours a highlight with a coloured circle after the opening marks: ==the default highlight==, ==🔴red==, ==🟠orange==, ==🟡yellow==, ==🟢green==, ==🔵blue== and ==🟣purple==.

A highlight can hold other formatting: ==🟡**bold**, *italic*, a link to [[Tour]] and `inline code`==, and ==🔵a highlight long enough to wrap onto a second line on a phone, so its background has to follow the text from one line to the next==.

- A list item with ==🟢a green highlight==
- [ ] A task with ==🔴a red highlight==

> A quote with ==🟣a purple highlight==.

> [!note] A callout
> With ==🟠an orange highlight== inside it.
