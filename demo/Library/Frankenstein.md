A scientist builds a life and then runs from it.

## Reading notes

The story arrives in three voices, one inside the next: Walton's letters to his sister, Victor's confession, and at the centre, the creature's own account. Each narrator is talking to someone who could still turn back.

> Beware; for I am fearless, and therefore powerful.

> [!question] Who is the monster?
> Victor never gives the creature a name. Refusing to name it is the first of his failures, and the one the others follow from.

## The creature

The creature is the most articulate person in the book. He learns to speak by listening at a cottage wall, reads *Paradise Lost* from a satchel found in the woods, and argues his case better than his maker ever argues his.
